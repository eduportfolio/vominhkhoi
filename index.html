Research Experience

Published · IJMSM Vol.3 Issue 1 · 2026
DOI: 10.71141/30485037/V3I1P123

Improving Performance of Electricity Theft Detection Using Combined Machine Learning Models with Real Applications in Vietnam

Ong Luong Tien Huy, Nguyen Quang Tung, Nguyen Hoang Nam Anh, Nguyen Quang Minh, Ngo Le Duc Anh, Vo Minh Khoi, Vu Xuan Manh

Electricity theft — a form of non-technical loss — causes significant economic and safety damage globally. This study proposes a machine learning pipeline applied to real smart meter data from Vietnam Electricity Corporation (EVN), covering 34,823 customers. The pipeline handles missing values, normalizes features, extracts statistical consumption patterns, and balances the skewed class distribution via over/undersampling. Multiple classifiers (SVM, KNN, Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Naïve Bayes) are benchmarked, with the proposed combined model achieving up to 99% accuracy — outperforming AES and HWOA-CSO baselines across accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and detection rate metrics.

Personal Projects

FEATURED PROJECT

PhysioTrack — Smart Physical Rehabilitation & Bio-Feedback Device

Designed and built a wearable, automated rehabilitation device to assist patients in post-injury joint recovery. The system integrates flex sensors and surface electromyography (sEMG) modules with an ESP32 microcontroller to capture real-time muscle engagement and motion data. Developed custom digital signal processing (DSP) algorithms to filter noise, calculate precise joint angles, and stream bio-feedback metrics to an interactive dashboard, allowing patients and physical therapists to track recovery progress dynamically.
